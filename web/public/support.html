WearBloom Support

Questions about your closet, a preview, your account or a subscription? We’re here to help.

Contact us

Email contact@monbonami.fr. Include your app version and a description of what happened.

Please do not attach personal photos, purchase receipts, passwords or Sign in with Apple tokens.

Subscriptions

Restore Purchases and subscription support are available in WearBloom under Profile & Settings.

You can also manage your App Store subscriptions directly with Apple.

Privacy controls

You can change AI and diagnostics permissions, manage reference photos or delete your account in Profile & Settings.

For privacy questions, email contact@monbonami.fr.

Response time

We aim to respond within two business days. Give us enough context to reproduce the issue, but never send sensitive credentials.
