WearBloom Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026.

WearBloom processes the photos and garment images you choose to provide to create and store your private looks. They are not made public unless you explicitly share a result.

What we collect

We process selected photos and generated results, an anonymous or Apple-linked account identifier, subscription and generation status, structured product interactions, and diagnostic information. We do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

How we use it

We use this information to provide image generation, keep your library private, enforce allowances, operate subscriptions, understand product reliability, and prevent abuse. Analytics and crash reports exclude photos, free-form notes, prompts, tokens, and secrets.

Deletion

You can delete your WearBloom data in Profile & Settings. Deletion queues removal of associated private files. App Store subscription cancellation is managed separately through Apple.

Contact

Privacy requests: privacy@wearbloom.app.
