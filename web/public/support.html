WearBloom Support

Need help with your closet, a preview, your account, or a subscription?

Contact us

Email contact@monbonami.fr. Include your app version and a description of what happened. Do not attach personal photos, purchase receipts, passwords, or Sign in with Apple tokens.

Subscriptions

Restore Purchases and subscription support are available in WearBloom under Profile & Settings. You can also manage your App Store subscriptions.

Privacy and deletion

You can change AI and diagnostics permissions or delete your account in Profile & Settings. For privacy questions, email contact@monbonami.fr.
