WearBloom Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026.

This policy explains how WearBloom collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use the WearBloom iPhone app and service.

Information you choose to provide

  • Photos and generated images. Reference photos, garment photos, and requested outfit previews.
  • Account information. An automatically created private account identifier and, if you optionally link Sign in with Apple, the Apple account identifier and email address Apple provides (which may be a private relay address).
  • Closet and look information. Garment names and categories, saved looks, generation status, allowances, and feedback you submit about a preview.
  • Purchases. Subscription product, status, and expiration information supplied by Apple. WearBloom does not receive your full payment-card details.

Information collected with your permission

AI processing. Before any selected personal data is sent for third-party AI processing, the app asks for your explicit permission. If allowed, WearBloom uploads the selected reference and garment photos to its private server and shares the inputs with OpenAI to classify garments and create the preview you requested. You can withdraw this permission in Settings. Withdrawing permission stops future third-party AI processing but does not delete previously created items; use the in-app deletion controls for that.

Optional analytics and diagnostics. If you enable “Share diagnostics and usage” in Settings, WearBloom sends limited product-interaction data to PostHog and crash, performance, and reliability data to Sentry. These events exclude photos, image contents, prompts, closet names, Apple tokens, and free-form user content. This permission is off by default and can be withdrawn in Settings at any time.

How information is used

We use information to provide and secure the app, create requested previews, store your private library, operate subscriptions and allowances, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, and—only when you opt in—understand reliability and product usage. We do not sell personal information, use advertising identifiers, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Service providers and protection

WearBloom uses Apple for sign-in and purchases, OpenAI for requested AI image processing, PostHog for optional analytics, Sentry for optional diagnostics, and infrastructure providers to host the private service. Providers receive only the information needed for their role. We require providers to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law and not use it for their independent advertising. Their processing is also governed by their applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Your working library is stored on your device. Items used with online AI features are also stored privately on WearBloom’s server so the requested service can operate. They are not published or visible to other users.

  • Account records and active private files are kept until you delete the related item or your account, unless a longer period is legally required.
  • When you delete your account in Settings, active account records are deleted and private-file cleanup is queued. Operational and disaster-recovery backups expire within 8 days.
  • Optional PostHog product analytics may be retained for up to 84 months, which is the current default retention period for the WearBloom PostHog project.
  • Optional Sentry crash and diagnostic data are retained for no more than 12 months.
  • Service providers may keep limited security or processing records for the periods in their applicable terms or when required by law.

Deleting a WearBloom account does not cancel an App Store subscription. You can manage or cancel it in the app or in your App Store subscription settings.

Your choices

In Profile & Settings you can withdraw AI-processing permission, opt in or out of diagnostics and analytics, manage reference photos, manage your App Store subscription, and permanently delete your WearBloom account and associated data. You can also contact us to ask a privacy question or exercise a right available in your location.

Children

WearBloom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child provided information.
